How it works

Three files. Ten minutes. Yours forever.

You get a tiny GitHub repo. Add your free Gemini key as a secret, edit one JSON file. Monday mornings, you get a report.

Clone the repo

“Use this template” on your own GitHub. One Python file, no dependencies, no accounts to create besides a free Google AI Studio key.

Edit config.json

Your brand, your competitors, your 5 prompts. Examples included. Commit.

Report every Monday

GitHub Actions runs at 08:00 UTC, generates an HTML report, mails it to you. Also saved as a workflow artifact for free week-over-week history.

Questions people actually ask

Fair enough.

Why only Gemini and not ChatGPT or Claude?

Because API calls to OpenAI, Anthropic and Perplexity cost real money per report, and I’m launching with zero budget. Gemini has a generous free tier, so I can keep the lifetime price at $19. Once there’s revenue, I’ll add engines — existing buyers get them included.

Is Gemini really enough? What about ChatGPT?

Honest answer: no, one engine is not everything. But Google Gemini also powers Google’s AI Overviews, which replace organic clicks on billions of searches. Tracking Gemini is tracking the search engine most of your prospects still start their day on. ChatGPT visibility matters too — it’s on the roadmap.

How is this different from Profound or Otterly?

Profound starts around $500 a month and shut off its starter plan in September. Otterly starts at $29 a month and charges extra for Gemini specifically. AskVisible is $19 one-time, Gemini-first. If your budget is "I just want to see if Google’s AI knows I exist", this is for you.

What if Gemini never mentions my brand?

Then the report tells you so, every week, with the actual snippet of what it says instead. Each report also includes one “fix hint” — the kind of page or citation that might change that. No guarantees, but at least you’ll know where you stand.

When does the first report arrive?

As soon as you’ve set up the repo and triggered the workflow once (there’s a “Run workflow” button in the Actions tab). After that, every Monday at 08:00 UTC.

Why self-hosted and not a hosted dashboard?

Three reasons: (1) your API key stays on your account — I never see your prompts or your usage. (2) It keeps the price at $19 forever instead of “$19/mo until I run out of margin”. (3) The repo is yours — if I vanish tomorrow, your reports keep landing on Monday.

Do I need to know Python / GitHub?

You need a GitHub account and you need to be comfortable clicking “New Secret” and editing a JSON file. If you’ve ever deployed a static site, you’ll be done in 10 minutes. If “git” is a foreign word, this isn’t for you yet.

Who built this?

One person, in public, on a weekend. If you want the full build log, follow @kaiversum on Bluesky.

Refunds?

If the first report lands in your inbox and you hate it, email back and I’ll refund you. No forms, no process.
